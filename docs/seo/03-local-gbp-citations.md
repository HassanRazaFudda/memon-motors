### Risk: the name "Memon Motors Melbourne"

Google's guidelines say the profile name must be the business's real-world name as it appears on signage and stationery, and that adding location or descriptors that are not part of the name can lead to suspension. The business card, the ABN business name and the website all say **"Memon Motors"** with no "Melbourne". Directory sites (Birdeye, RepairBook, Localsearch, Calso) copied "Memon Motors Melbourne" from Google. **Action for the owner:** check the shopfront sign. If it says only "Memon Motors", ask to change the name in the dashboard to match. If "Melbourne" is really on the sign, leave it. This is a judgement for the owner, not something to change blindly, because name edits can trigger re-verification.

## What drives local (Maps) ranking

Google states three factors: **relevance** (how well the profile matches the search), **distance** (from the searcher or the named area to the business), **prominence** (how well known the business is: reviews, links, citations, general web presence).

For Memon Motors: relevance is fine and fixable (categories, services, description). Distance is fixed. **Prominence is the gap.** The leader in the "mechanic truganina" pack has about six times the reviews at the same 4.9 rating. This is why the plan below is review- and consistency-led, not tricks.
