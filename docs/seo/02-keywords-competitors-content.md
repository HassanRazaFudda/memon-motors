# Keyword research, competitors and content plan

Data gathered 31 Oct 2026. Read `01-audit.md` first for sources and limits. **Search volumes and keyword difficulty were not obtainable without paid tools or a Google Ads account, so none are quoted.** Evidence tiers used below:

- **A** = phrase appears in Google AU autocomplete (people really type it) **and** a competitor ranks for it in the live SERP.
- **B** = appears in autocomplete only.
- **C** = no autocomplete evidence. Qualitative only.

## What the research actually showed

1. **Spell it out: "roadworthy certificate", not "RWC".** Autocomplete for `rwc truganina` returns rugby results (`rwc wins`, `rwc history`). `roadworthy certificate truganina` and `roadworthy truganina` return clean local suggestions. Lead with "roadworthy certificate" and use RWC as the secondary term.
2. **Australians say "mechanic", "car service", "car repairs", "logbook service", "roadworthy".** US-style "auto repair" collides with Melbourne, Florida in autocomplete and SERPs. Do not target "auto repair Melbourne", "auto engineer" or "best car mechanics in Australia".
3. **Do not chase "Melbourne".** `best mechanic in melbourne` is a citywide fight a one-workshop business cannot win and does not need to. The money is hyper-local: Truganina plus the five suburbs within 13 minutes.
4. **"Price" is the follow-up question.** `roadworthy certificate truganina price` is in Google's "people also search for", and cost questions dominate roadworthy autocomplete. Friends Auto Solutions' RWC page quotes no price and has no FAQ, which is an opening for plain, honest cost guidance.
5. **"Mobile mechanic [suburb]" is everywhere** in autocomplete. Memon Motors is not documented as mobile, so it is not targeted.
6. **Rental searches are dominated by big brands** (Sixt, Skyscanner, Expedia, Turo). Small local operators (Thar Motors, Shubh Motor Group) do appear, so a genuine local page can compete on specific phrases, but only with facts the owner has not yet supplied.
7. **`uber car rental truganina` and `uber car rental tarneit` are real autocomplete phrases.** Rideshare-driver rentals are a distinct market. Whether Memon Motors offers them is unknown, so there is no page for it. Ask the owner.

### Google AI Mode answer (one query, observed 31 Oct 2026)

Query: *who is a reliable mechanic in Truganina for a roadworthy certificate and car rental.* The answer named **Friends Auto Solutions** as the "premier choice" because it "uniquely" offers roadworthy certificates **and** rentals from one place, then Truganina Auto Repairs, Mel Auto Care and DMD Automotives, plus Sixt/Budget/Hertz for rental. **Memon Motors was not mentioned**, even though it offers exactly that combination. The lesson is not a ranking trick. The AI summary rewarded sites that state the combination plainly, show a licence, and carry large review counts. The site now states the combination plainly in the hero, title, home FAQ and About.

### How Memon Motors can realistically win

1. **Trust and honesty content they lack:** plain cost guidance, a visible "no work without your approval" promise, the official roadworthy rules cited to Transport Victoria. Done on the RWC page.
2. **Show the licence** once verified (owner input). This is the single biggest gap versus every roadworthy competitor.
3. **Review volume.** Rating is already equal. Prominence in Maps is driven by review count and recency, so this is the main lever.
4. **Own the rental+repair+RWC combination** in plain language (done), and give rental specifics once supplied.
5. **Clean citations** so Google and AI engines see one business, not two addresses.

## GEO: what is evidence-based

No ranking signal for any AI product is assumed. What is supported by what was observed and by how these systems are documented to retrieve local information:

1. **Consistent entity facts everywhere** (name, address, phone, hours). Currently broken on at least five third-party listings (see `03-local-gbp-citations.md`).
2. **Plain statements of what the business is** ("roadworthy certificates, mechanical repairs, car rentals and vehicle sales from one Truganina workshop"). Done.
3. **Verifiable credentials on the page** (licensed tester number). Pending owner.
4. **Third-party corroboration:** reviews, directories, council listings.
5. **Cited, accurate answers** to common questions. Done for roadworthy rules, with Transport Victoria sources linked.
6. **Test, don't assume.** Re-run the same queries monthly (below) and record results.
