# Social media strategy and video advertising concepts

## 1. Ground rules (read before producing anything)

1. **Real beats synthetic wherever the business is shown.** AI-generated footage must never pass as Memon Motors' workshop, team, customers or results. Anything that shows "us" (Sadiq, mechanics at work, the shopfront, a customer's car) is filmed for real on a phone. AI generation is used only for **illustrative** shots: a macro of a worn brake disc, tyre tread close-ups, animated diagrams, abstract brand motion.
2. **Every video has at least one real shot.** In each concept below, scenes are tagged **[REAL]** (film it) or **[AI]** (generate it). A video that is 100% AI reads as generic and, for a trust business, undermines the message.
3. **Label AI content.** TikTok, YouTube and Meta each have rules requiring disclosure of realistic AI-generated or altered content. Check and follow each platform's current rules when posting. Never generate fake people giving testimonials.
4. **No unverifiable claims.** Same list as the website: no "licensed", "best", "cheapest", "specialist", warranties or years in trade until confirmed. Say "4.9★ from 100+ Google reviews" only while it's true, and update it.
5. **Music:** business accounts must use commercially licensed audio (Meta Sound Collection, TikTok Commercial Music Library, YouTube Audio Library). Don't use trending chart songs in ads.
6. **Customer consent.** Get written OK before filming a customer, their face, number plate or car. Blur plates by default.

## 2. Brand kit for video

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Colours | Black `#0A0A0A` background, red `#B31E20` for CTAs and highlights, gold `#C59129 → #F0C04B` stripe as the signature transition/underline, white text |
| Type | Archivo Black/ExtraBold UPPERCASE for on-screen headlines (max ~6 words per card), Inter for captions |
| Logo | Gold winged-M badge, bottom-right watermark at ~8% width; full badge on end card |
| End card (every video, 2–3 s) | Black, gold stripe wipe, badge, "MEMON MOTORS · TRUGANINA", "Call 0469 042 720 · WhatsApp us" |
| Look | Low-key, high-contrast workshop lighting, shallow depth of field, slow push-ins. Grade towards cool neutrals with warm practical lights |
| Safe zones | 9:16 at 1080×1920. Keep text out of the bottom ~20% and right ~15% (platform UI) |
| Captions | Burned-in subtitles on every video (most viewers watch muted) |
| Voice | Calm, plain Australian English. Not shouty, no fake urgency |

**AI generation style suffix** (append to every [AI] prompt below):
> *cinematic automotive commercial, dark workshop, black and deep red colour palette with warm gold highlights, low-key lighting, shallow depth of field, anamorphic look, realistic textures, no logos, no brand badges, no text, no number plates, 9:16 vertical*

## 3. Platforms and roles

| Platform | Role | Cadence | Formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Local community, reviews, offers, Marketplace-age audience. The existing page (~566 followers) | 3 posts/week | Reels, photo posts, Google review screenshots (with permission) |
| Instagram | Visual brand, before/after, reels | 3 posts/week + stories | Reels 9:16, carousels 4:5 |
| TikTok | Reach with younger drivers; educational "signs your…" content | 2–3/week | 9:16, 15–35 s |
| YouTube Shorts | Searchable how-to and explainers; long shelf life | 1–2/week (repurpose) | 9:16, ≤60 s |
| Google Business Profile | Not social, but posts and photos show in Maps | 1 post/week | Photo + short text |

**Content mix:** 40% education (signs, tips, explainers), 25% behind the scenes (real jobs, the team), 20% proof (reviews, customer thank-yous, with consent), 15% direct offer (book a service, tyre quote via WhatsApp).

### V2. Problem/solution: "That squeal isn't nothing" (brakes)

1. **Objective:** Leads (calls/WhatsApp) for brake inspections.
2. **Audience:** Same radius, all ages with a licence; retarget page visitors.
3. **Concept:** Sound-led. Open on the squeal, then teach the three warning signs, then show the fix is simple and quoted first.
4. **Hook (0–2 s):** Black screen, a sharp brake squeal, text slams in: "HEAR THAT?"
5. **Script / VO:** "That squeal is often your brake pads' wear indicator telling you they're getting thin. Grinding is worse: it can mean metal on metal. A soft pedal or the car pulling to one side? Get it checked. At Memon Motors we measure your pads and discs, show you what's worn, and quote before we touch it."
6. **Scenes:**
   1. (0–2 s) [AI] Extreme close-up of a car wheel slowing at dusk, brake disc glowing faintly through the spokes.
   2. (2–6 s) [AI] Macro: a worn brake pad next to a new one, side by side.
   3. (6–10 s) [AI] Animated diagram: pad, disc, calliper in gold line-art on black.
   4. (10–15 s) [REAL] Mechanic measuring a disc with a micrometer.
   5. (15–19 s) [REAL] Mechanic showing the worn pad to camera.
   6. (19–22 s) End card + "Brake check: WhatsApp us".

   - S2: "Macro product shot, a heavily worn brake pad beside a new brake pad on a black steel workbench, hard side light revealing texture" + suffix.
   - S3: "Minimal technical animation, gold line drawing of a disc brake assembly (rotor, calliper, pads) assembling on a pure black background" + suffix.
8. **VO:** As above.
9. **On-screen text:** "HEAR THAT?" / "SQUEAL = PADS GETTING THIN" / "GRINDING = GET IT CHECKED NOW" / "MEASURED. SHOWN. QUOTED FIRST."

## 7. Production plan

1. **One shoot day at the workshop** (2–3 hours, a phone on a gimbal is enough): roller door, hoist, every [REAL] shot above, 10 portraits of Sadiq and the team, exterior with signage, 20 stills of real work. This one session feeds the website, the Google profile, social and every ad.
2. **AI shots:** generate with a current text-to-video tool that allows commercial use, using the prompts above. Generate 3–4 takes per shot and pick the most realistic. Reject any take with garbled text, logos or distorted hands.
3. **Edit** in CapCut or Premiere using the brand kit; burn in captions; export 1080×1920 H.264.
4. **Review checklist before posting:** claims are confirmed, AI shots don't depict "our" workshop, captions are spelled right, the phone number is correct, music is from a commercial library, AI disclosure is applied where required.
