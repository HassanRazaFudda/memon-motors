## 10. National "Top Tier" / Best-Designed Players (industry benchmark, not direct competitors)

For reference on what a polished, high-converting website in this space looks like:

| Business | What stands out |
|---|---|
| **Lube Mobile** (lubemobile.com.au) | Australia's largest mobile mechanic network, operating since 1982. Postcode/suburb-based booking widget on the homepage, itemised/upfront quoting with no work starting without approval, 12-month/20,000km nationwide warranty, flexible payment (Afterpay/Humm/Zip, EFTPOS, cards), clear FAQ block answering pricing/timing/coverage objections. |
| **Mobile Tyre Shop** (mobiletyreshop.com.au) | Order tyres online, fitting included — "never have to leave the couch" positioning. Simple, conversion-first value prop. |
| **Australia's Detailing Group** (austdg.com.au) | Mobile detailing with packaged, fixed-price service tiers — good reference for transparent pricing pages (not independently re-verified this session, site was JS-heavy/slow to load). |
| **Friends Auto Solutions** (local) | See competitor section above — most relevant real-world template since it's the same service mix and same suburb. |

**Common patterns across all of them that Memon Motors currently lacks:**
1. A real website (booking + pricing + service area pages) instead of relying solely on directory listings.
2. Visible/itemised pricing — builds trust before the customer calls.
3. A short, simple booking form (suburb/postcode → service → time).
4. Dedicated local-SEO pages per suburb served (Truganina, Point Cook, Tarneit, Hoppers Crossing, Werribee, Laverton), matching what's already on the Facebook page's service-area list.
5. Warranty/guarantee statement displayed prominently.

## 11. Key Opportunity

Memon Motors has a stronger rating (4.9★/100+ reviews) than most of its direct local competitors but is the only one among them without an official website — all traffic currently depends on third-party directories and word-of-mouth/Facebook. Building a simple site (home, services, RWC, rentals, pricing indicator, suburb pages, Google Maps embed, click-to-call/WhatsApp) modeled on Friends Auto Solutions' structure would be the fastest way to convert the existing reputation into direct bookings.
